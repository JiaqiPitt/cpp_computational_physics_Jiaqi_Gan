Exercise 3
==========

I just implemented my own exponential function using 100 terms of the Taylor
series. Its results agreed with std::exp to the displayed precision for
most of the tested values. Small differences appeared at x = -10 and
x = -8 because the alternating Taylor terms cause some loss of floating
point precision.

For custom_exp, nm showed `T my_exp(double)`, I guess it means that the
function was defined inside the executable. The custom executable had no
undefined _exp symbol. For system_exp, nm showed `U _exp`, basically means that
the function had to be obtained from an external system library.
