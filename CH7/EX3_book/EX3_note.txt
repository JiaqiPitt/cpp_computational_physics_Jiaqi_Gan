Exercise 7.3 notes

I ran a Metropolis chain in 3D velocity space, in units of sqrt(kT/m), with target exp(-|u|^2/2). Each step adds a Gaussian random number of width STEP to every component, and the move is accepted with probability min(1, exp(-(|u'|^2 - |u|^2)/2)). The speed |u| then follows the Maxwell distribution, and each component should be a unit Gaussian.

Settings: seed 12345, 1000 burn-in steps, then 100000 samples recorded every 10 steps. The errors on the means come from 100 batch means, since neighbouring samples are correlated.

To choose STEP I tried a few values and looked at the correlation between neighbouring recorded speeds:

 STEP   acceptance   lag-1 corr   chi^2/87
 0.5      0.69         0.31         58
 1.0      0.45         0.15        101
 1.5      0.28         0.20        116
 2.0      0.18         0.33        164
 2.5      0.12         0.48        185

With small steps almost every move is accepted but the chain moves slowly. With big steps most moves are rejected. STEP = 1.0 gave the lowest correlation, so I used that.

Output with STEP = 1.0:
proposal width 1.00, acceptance rate 0.450
recorded 100000 samples, one every 10 steps, after 1000 burn-in steps
lag-1 correlation of recorded speeds: 0.151
<u>   = 1.5961 +- 0.0027   exact sqrt(8/pi) = 1.5958
<u^2> = 3.0033 +- 0.0102   exact 3
speed histogram chi^2 / ndof = 101.0 / 87

Both means agree with the exact values within 1 sigma. chi^2/ndof is 1.16, a little above 1. That fits with the samples still being slightly correlated, because chi^2 assumes independent entries.
