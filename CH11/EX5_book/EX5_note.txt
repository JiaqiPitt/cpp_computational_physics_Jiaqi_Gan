Exercise 11.5 notes

The equations of the spinning top, with u = cos theta:
  du/dt   = sqrt(f(u)),   f(u) = (1 - u^2)(alpha - beta u) - (b - a u)^2
  dphi/dt = (b - a u) / (1 - u^2)
  dpsi/dt = I1 a / I3 - u (b - a u) / (1 - u^2)

The catch is the square root. u swings between two roots of f and du/dt has to change sign at each of them, which sqrt can't do, so integrating it as written gets stuck at the first turning point. Differentiating (du/dt)^2 = f(u) gives d^2u/dt^2 = f'(u)/2, which has no sign problem. I integrated u, du/dt, phi and psi with RKIntegrator and an AdaptiveRKStepper (tolerance 1e-10), and got f'(u) from f.partial(U).

Toy top: M = 0.1 kg, l = 4 cm, I1 = 1.6e-4 kg m^2, I3 = 6e-5 kg m^2, spin 150 rad/s. Every run starts with theta' = 0, so u starts at a turning point:
  cusps: let go at rest at theta = 30 deg
  waves: phi' = +2 rad/s at theta = 50 deg
  loops: phi' = -2 rad/s at theta = 70 deg

Results over the first second:
          theta range (deg)   nutation period (s)   mean precession (rad/s)
  cusps   30.00 - 35.55       0.129529              4.67674
  waves   50.00 - 54.37       0.122413              4.53939
  loops   70.00 - 82.85       0.115428              4.40665

Checks: u stays between the two roots of f to 6 digits, (du/dt)^2 - f(u) is never more than 6e-8 /s^2, and the period and precession rate agree with 2 int du/sqrt(f) to every digit printed. For that integral I put u = u1 + (u2 - u1) sin^2 s, so there's no singularity at the ends.
